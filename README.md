Komputopedia is a small, curiosity-driven initiative for learning and sharing computer science and AI. It began as an educational YouTube channel by three scholarship students and now continues as a one-person, AI-enabled studio: the ideas and judgement are human, and AI helps with the execution.

| Division | What it does |
|---|---|
| **KomputopedAI** | Research and engineering, run as a one-person organisation supported by AI |
| **KomputoMedia** | Short, simple explainers about computer science and AI for Indonesian students ([Instagram @komputopedia](https://www.instagram.com/komputopedia/)) |
| **Komputopedia Kids** | Playful learning for children (coming later) |
